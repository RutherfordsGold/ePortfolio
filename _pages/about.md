I am an Honours student in Materials Science at the University of Cape Town, with a BSc. majoring in Chemistry and Biochemistry. I have a strong interest in the relationship between structure and function, particularly in material systems. Through my academic and laboratory experience, I have developed skills in experimental design, data analysis, and problem-solving, and I am keen to apply these while continuing to develop new techniques.

Interests and Sport
=========
In my spare time I enjoy spending time in the garden, growing a wide variety of different plants ranging from vegetables and ornamental plants to succulents and cacti. When I am not outdoors I am inside doing art, particularly with pencil drawing and digital art. For sport, I enjoy horse riding, cycling, hiking, climbing and running my dogs in agility.

Equestrian
==============
I am a keen horse rider and enjoy participating in disciplines like show jumping, English mounted games, dressage and carriage driving. I am on the committee of the South African Mounted Games Association (SAMGA) and Western Province Mounted Games Association (WPMGA), and have been involved in helping with the organisation of various events, such as Nations Team Championship 2023 hosted by South Africa and with a demonstration at the NAMPO Expo in 2025.

Trickddog and Agility
========
Dog agility and training is a big part of my life, I started agaility training in 2024 with my two Jack Russell Terriers with the Fish Hoek Agility Dogs club. During this time I've competed at many competions hosted by the South African Dog Agility Association (SADAA) as well as the Western Province Regional Trials 2024 and Boland Regional Trials in 2023 and 2024. Aside from agility and jumping, I also spend a lot of time teaching my dogs how to do tricks and have recieved the KUSA Elementary Trickdog title for both my dogs, as well as the other KUSA speciality titles "Barkour" and "My 2025 Trick Journey".
